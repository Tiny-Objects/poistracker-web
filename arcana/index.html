Arcana Desk · iPhone

A card each morning, and a sky of your own.

One tarot card a day. You turn it over, and it stays turned: the same card until midnight, with what it means and what it asks. No shuffling again until you like the answer.

Underneath it, your day, read from the sky as it stands over your own birth chart. Not a sun sign column. Your transits, worked out from the date, the time and the place you were born.

What is inside

  • The card of the dayIts meaning, what it gives, what it costs, and one question to sit with.
  • Readings you lay out yourselfFifteen spreads, or build your own. Ask a question or draw without one.
  • Your birth chart in fullPlanets, houses, aspects, the Ascendant and the Midheaven, all computed on the phone.
  • NumerologyLife path, expression, soul urge and personal year, from your name and birth date.
  • Your circleAdd the people you care about and read the connection between two charts, in words instead of a percentage.
  • A journalEvery reading kept by date, with the card of each day and room to write.
  • Learn Tarot and Learn AstrologyWritten from a shelf of books rather than from memory, and it names a disagreement where one exists.

Your own deck

Photograph your own deck, card by card, and the app uses it everywhere: in the daily card, in readings, and on the home screen widget.

Widgets

Three sizes. The card of the day, face down until you turn it, with the back of the deck you chose. The large one carries your horoscope.

Mystic

The written library is free. Mystic adds the AI readings that answer a question in your own words, the full daily horoscope, and the deeper chart work. Monthly or yearly, cancel anytime.

Arcana Desk is for reflection, not prediction. It is not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.
